Average Cost of Waterproofing a Basement in Burnsville, MN.

Homeowners searching for an average cost want a number, but waterproofing prices vary widely. This guide explains what moves the price and how to read a quote.

By the Burnsville 24/7 Plumbing team | Published November 16, 2026
.

.

.Call Now.

Why there is no honest average.

A waterproofing job might be a downspout extension or a full interior drain system with a sump. Those two projects share a name and almost nothing else. An average of them tells you nothing about your basement.

A better question is what is causing the water and which fix addresses it. The price follows from that.

What drives the price.

Factor.Why it matters.
Source of the water.Surface water, groundwater and plumbing leaks need different fixes.
Length of wall or floor edge.More linear feet means more labor and pipe.
Finished or unfinished basement.Finished walls and floors raise the cost of access.
Sump system.Pump quality, a backup and the discharge line add cost.
Exterior work.Digging around the foundation is a larger project.

Get at least two written quotes that name the work in detail. A quote that says only waterproofing is not a quote. It should list the drains, the sump, the pump, the discharge and the restoration of the floor.

The low-cost fixes first.

Gutters and downspouts.

Extend them several feet from the house.

Grading.

Soil should slope away from the foundation.

Window wells.

Clear them and check the drains.

Sump test.

Pour water into the pit and confirm the pump runs.

These are cheap and often make a difference. A basement that floods because a downspout dumps at the wall will not be fixed by an interior drain.

The interior drain and sump system.

When groundwater pressure pushes water up through the floor edge, an interior drain at the footing collects it and carries it to a sump pit. A pump moves the water outside. This is plumbing work, and it is the main thing a plumber contributes to waterproofing.

The pump matters as much as the drain. A cheap pump that fails in a storm defeats the system. See sump pump repair for what a reliable setup looks like, and basement waterproofing for the plumber's side of the job.

Is it water or is it a leak?.

A damp basement is sometimes a plumbing leak, not groundwater. Check the water meter with every fixture off. If it keeps moving, the water is from a pipe. A leak detection visit finds it far more cheaply than a waterproofing system.

How to compare quotes.

Ask what causes the water.

A good contractor explains the diagnosis.

Compare scope.

Same linear feet, same pump, same discharge.

Check the warranty.

Read what it covers and what voids it.

Verify the license.

Plumbing work needs a licensed plumber.

Red flags in a waterproofing quote.

Be wary of a quote that arrives without anyone looking at the basement. Be wary of a quote that names a product but not a diagnosis, and of a quote that says everything needs to be dug up. A good contractor explains where the water comes from before pricing the fix.

Be wary of pressure to sign the same day. Waterproofing is a project you can plan. A written quote with a clear scope will still be good next week, and a second opinion is cheap compared with the work.

A sensible order for spending money.

Spend on the cheapest fix that addresses the diagnosed cause first. Extend the downspouts, regrade the soil and clean the window wells before you price any system that involves digging or cutting concrete. If water still arrives after those steps, you have learned something useful about the source.

Next, make sure the sump system is sound. A tired pump in a good pit is a small repair. A good pump in a pit with no drain connection to the floor edge is a design problem. Only after those steps does it make sense to price interior drains along the walls.

Step.What it costs you.What it tells you.
Downspouts and grading.Little money, a weekend of work.Whether surface water is the main cause.
Sump test and pump repair.A service call and parts.Whether the system can keep up in a storm.
Camera or meter check.A short visit.Whether the water comes from a pipe.
Interior drain tile.The largest cost of the four.Whether groundwater pressure is the real source.

Local rules and sources.

The City of Burnsville's Water and Sewer Maintenance page covers public sewer and water guidance. It does not give waterproofing prices, and any contractor who quotes a flat citywide price is guessing.

Rules on where a sump pump may discharge vary by city. Ask Burnsville Building Inspections at 952-895-4444 before connecting a sump to a storm or sanitary line.

Minnesota's Department of Labor and Industry offers a Check a License tool for plumbers. Use it for the plumbing part of any project.

The plumbing parts of a waterproofing job, such as sump discharge and drains, are held to the Minnesota Plumbing Code, the state standard in Minnesota Rules chapter 4714.

We answer the emergency line 24/7 from Burnsville and send plumbers to Lakeville and Prior Lake as well, so the same advice applies if you live just outside the city. If the job turns out to be bigger than a homeowner fix, call and describe what you see.
