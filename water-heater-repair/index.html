If the water heater has stopped making hot water or is leaking, the first step is the same in both cases: find the shutoff. Turn the cold supply valve above the tank to stop water from entering, and for a gas unit turn the gas control to pilot or off. If water is spreading, shut off the main water valve and call. We answer the line 24/7, and a leaking tank is treated as a same-day job.

A heater that is simply not heating is less urgent but still a daily problem. Most of the time the cause is a part that can be replaced in a single visit: a thermostat, a heating element, a thermocouple, an igniter or a gas valve.

When a leaking water heater is an emergency.

Do this before we arrive.

  • Close the cold water valve on the supply line above the heater..
  • Turn the heater power or gas control off before you touch anything near wet electrical parts..
  • Move boxes and stored items away from the tank and mop water away from the basement floor drain..
  • If water is coming from the relief valve discharge pipe, call and do not cap or plug it..

Water heater leaks are dispatched same day, including evenings and weekends. A tank leaking from its body cannot be patched and usually means replacement.

Repair or replace.

Repair is the right answer when the failing part is inexpensive and the tank is in good shape. Replacement makes sense when the tank itself leaks, when the heater is near the end of its useful life or when repair costs are heading toward half of what a new unit would cost. A plumber should show you the data plate with the manufacture date and explain the reasoning.

If you decide replacement is next, see water heater replacement for what that visit involves, or tankless water heater service if you want to compare tank and tankless options.

Hard Burnsville water and water heater life.

The City of Burnsville's 2025 water report lists tap water hardness at 25 to 26 grains per gallon. That is very hard water, and heating it causes minerals to precipitate onto the tank bottom and electric elements. Sediment in a tank means louder popping when the burner runs, less efficient heating and an element that burns out sooner.

A yearly tank flush and an anode rod check slow the damage. Homes with a softener upstream typically see less scale, but the softener must be set correctly. Our water softener page covers what the settings need to match.

Permits and licensing.

Burnsville's plumbing permit page lists water heaters among projects that can be permitted online, and Minnesota requires plumbing work to be done by licensed plumbers or the homeowner who owns and occupies the home. You can check any plumber with the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry's Check a License tool.

The standard behind the repair.

Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, the state plumbing code, sets the standard for relief valves, discharge piping, venting and combustion air on water heaters. A repair that touches any of those parts should meet it, and a plumber should say so when it does.
