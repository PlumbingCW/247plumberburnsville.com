About Burnsville 24/7 Plumbing.

A 24/7 plumbing line for Burnsville and the four cities around it, run from Nicollet Avenue.

24/7 emergency line

Burnsville 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing dispatch service based at 14150 Nicollet Avenue in Burnsville, Minnesota. We answer the phone every hour of every day and send a plumber to Burnsville, Savage, Lakeville, Prior Lake and Rosemount.

Plumbing problems rarely arrive at a convenient time. A pipe that bursts at two in the morning and a water heater that fails on a holiday weekend are the calls this business is built around.

How a call works.

Answer.

A person answers the line, listens and asks what is happening and where.

Advise.

We tell you what to do while you wait, such as closing the main valve or stopping use of a drain.

Dispatch.

Urgent calls go out the same day, including nights and weekends.

Price and fix.

You hear a clear price before the work starts, and the plumber explains what failed.

The advice step matters more than people expect. A homeowner who closes the main valve in the first minutes limits the water damage before anyone arrives. We would rather spend two minutes on the phone than leave a house flooding while a truck is on the road.

What we focus on.

The site covers 22 services, from emergency drain and sewer work to water heaters, leak detection, fixtures and sump pumps. The services page groups them by problem so you can find the one that matches what you see.

Most of our calls come from a short list of causes, and the pages on this site follow that list. Hard water wears out heaters and fixtures in this area, winter freezes split pipes, spring thaws overwhelm sump pumps and older drain lines clog with roots and scale. The pages on this site are written around those causes.

Where we work.

Our base is in Burnsville, and we also dispatch to Savage, Lakeville, Prior Lake and Rosemount. Each city page uses that city's own published water report, so the facts on it come from the people who run the water system.

What we will and will not claim.

This site does not show customer reviews, star ratings or testimonials, because we would rather show nothing than show something we cannot back up. It does not name individual plumbers, and it does not publish prices that vary by job.

What it does show is local information from primary sources. The water facts come from the City of Burnsville and neighboring cities' own reports, and the permit and licensing facts come from the city and the state.

Licensing and permits.

Minnesota licenses plumbers, and the Department of Labor and Industry lets anyone verify a license with its Check a License tool. We encourage you to use it on any plumber you consider, including us.

According to the City of Burnsville's Plumbing Permit page, some plumbing projects need a permit, and Building Inspections can be reached at 952-895-4444. Water heaters and water softeners are among the projects the city lists.

Pricing works the same way on every job. The plumber looks at the problem, tells you what it will cost and waits for your answer before starting. If the price is more than you expected, you can ask what drives it, or ask for a smaller first step.

What to have ready when you call.

The dispatcher will ask for the street address, what is happening and whether water is spreading. If you can, find the main water valve before you call. It is usually near the meter, close to the laundry room or the furnace.

Describe what you see and hear, not what you think is wrong. A hissing wall, a gurgling drain and a stained ceiling each tell the plumber something different. Photos taken before you start cleanup help later with insurance.

If a sewer backup is involved, keep people and pets away from the water, and do not run the washer or dishwasher. The City of Burnsville asks residents with a sewer backup to call Public Works at 952-895-4550 on weekdays and 9-1-1 evenings and weekends, so crews can check whether the city main is the cause.
