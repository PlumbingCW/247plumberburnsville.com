Prior Lake water: treated, but still hard.

The City of Prior Lake reports total hardness of 19.3 grains per gallon, and tells homeowners to use that figure to set a softener. Its water treatment facility, opened in 2009, removes iron and manganese and does not soften. A home softener in Prior Lake should therefore need less salt than one in a house with iron-heavy water, but it still has plenty of hardness to remove.

Summer demand and the water system.

The city says summer peak water use reaches about 7 million gallons a day, mostly from irrigation, against a plant capacity of 7.5 million gallons per day. That is a very high peak for a small city, and it tells you something practical: irrigation is a major load on Prior Lake plumbing in summer.

A sprinkler line that leaks or a backflow device that fails can waste water for weeks before anyone notices. If your bill spikes in July, our meter test and leak detection is the first step. Water line breaks between the meter and house are covered on water line repair.

Lake homes and basement water.

Prior Lake is a lake community, and a home near the water may sit close to the water table. A sump pump is the first defense against a wet basement, and it fails at the worst time. Our sump pump repair page covers testing, check valves and battery backups.

For recurring seepage, basement waterproofing explains what plumbing can fix and what needs another trade.
