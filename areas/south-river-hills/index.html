Where South River Hills is.

The City of Burnsville lists South River Hills as an organized neighborhood. The city describes it as bounded on the north and west by Highway 13, on the east by the Burnsville and Eagan border, and on the south by Cliff Road East.

The neighborhood holds an annual South River Hills Day event, according to the city's neighborhood page. That is a sign of an active neighborhood group, not a statement about anyone's plumbing.

City line or your line.

When water or sewage backs up, the cause may be in the house, in the private line to the street, or in the city main. The City of Burnsville's Public Works page tells residents with a sewer backup to call 952-895-4550 on weekdays and 9-1-1 in the evenings and weekends.

The city's page does not say who owns each section of pipe in every situation, so we do not guess. A camera inspection can show where a blockage or break sits, which makes the conversation with the city or a contractor much simpler.

Housing in the neighborhood.

The City of Burnsville is a mix of older and newer housing. A 2017 city planning presentation put about 56.0% of the city's housing units at 1980 or earlier, and the city does not publish a neighborhood by neighborhood breakdown in the documents we reviewed. We do not claim that older homes in South River Hills have a particular pipe material or defect.

Practical steps during a backup.

Stop the water.

Avoid using sinks, tubs and the washer while the drain is blocked.

Keep clear.

Keep people and pets away from any sewage and do not touch it with bare hands.

Call the right number.

Use the city number above for a city main problem, or call us to talk through a private line.

Take photos of the affected area for your records. If sewage is in the house, a cleanup contractor may be needed in addition to a plumber.

The plumbing standard.

Plumbing work in Minnesota follows the Minnesota Plumbing Code, Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, and the City of Burnsville handles plumbing permits and inspections. Ask any plumber how the work will meet the code before you agree to it.
