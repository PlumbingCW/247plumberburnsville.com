Where West Buck Hill is.

The City of Burnsville lists West Buck Hill as an organized neighborhood. The city describes it as bounded on the north by Rivage Lane and Lucerne Circle, on the east by North Bryant Avenue, on the south by East 158th Street, and on the west by County Road 5.

Our description follows the city's boundaries and does not try to define where the name is used outside them. If your address is near an edge, call and we can talk it through.

What the city has published about service lines.

The City of Burnsville's 2025 drinking water report includes a service line inventory, and it counts 17,004 non-lead service lines across the city. The report is the right place to check what the city knows about service lines.

We do not know the material of your individual service line. If it matters to you, ask the city about your address, and a plumber can confirm the material where the pipe enters the house.

Housing in the neighborhood.

The City of Burnsville is a mix of older and newer housing. A 2017 city planning presentation put about 56.0% of the city's housing units at 1980 or earlier, and the city does not publish a neighborhood by neighborhood breakdown in the documents we reviewed. Pipe material inside a house depends on when and how it was built or remodeled, so we do not assume copper, galvanized or plastic from a neighborhood name.

Questions to ask before a pipe project.

What is the pipe made of.

Ask the plumber to identify the material and show you.

Is a permit needed.

Building Inspections at 952-895-4444 can confirm what the city requires.

What is the scope.

Get the work described in writing, including what is opened and repaired afterward.

The plumbing standard.

Plumbing work in Minnesota follows the Minnesota Plumbing Code, Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, and the City of Burnsville handles plumbing permits and inspections. Ask any plumber how the work will meet the code before you agree to it.
