Where Cedarbridge and River's Edge is.

The City of Burnsville lists Cedarbridge/River's Edge as an organized neighborhood. The city describes it as bounded on the north by the Minnesota River Valley, on the east by Cedarbridge and McCool Drive East, on the south by Foxpoint Road, and on the west by Chatham Court North and surrounding parcels.

The Minnesota River forms Burnsville's northern border, according to general references on the city. We describe the area using the city's boundaries only.

Protecting pipes in a Minnesota winter.

Any Minnesota home can have a pipe freeze in a hard cold snap, especially in an exterior wall, an unheated space or a garage. We do not claim that homes in this neighborhood freeze more often than others.

A few habits lower the risk. Disconnect outdoor hoses, keep cabinet doors open on very cold nights under exterior-wall sinks, and let a faucet drip slightly if a pipe has frozen before.
