Burnsville drain cleaning starts with one question: is the clog in a single fixture or in the line they all share? A kitchen sink that gurgles by itself is a different job than a basement floor drain that bubbles when the upstairs toilet flushes. The line is open 24/7, so tell us which fixtures are affected and we can usually say on the phone how urgent it is.

While you wait, stop running water in the house, keep the washing machine and dishwasher off, and do not pour chemical drain opener into a line that is fully stopped. Standing chemicals sit in the pipe and burn the next person who works on it. Move rugs and stored boxes away from the floor drain, because a main line that finally releases can push water up fast.

When a clogged drain is an emergency.

Treat it as urgent if any of these are true.

  • Several fixtures are slow or backing up at once, which points to the main line rather than one trap..
  • Water or sewage is coming up through a basement floor drain, shower or tub..
  • The toilet bubbles or rises when you run the washer..
  • Nobody can use the only bathroom in the house..

Same-day and after-hours calls are normal for drain work. A blocked main line does not wait for office hours, and neither does our dispatch.

Signs the problem is bigger than one sink.

Gurgling after a flush.

Air pulled through a partly blocked line often shows up first as sounds from a tub or sink on the same branch.

Slow drains on every floor.

A single slow sink is usually a trap or tailpiece. Slowness everywhere points downstream.

Water in the lowest fixture.

The basement floor drain or lowest shower shows a main line problem before anything upstairs does.

Repeat clogs every few months.

Grease, roots or a belly in the pipe keep catching debris, and cable work only treats the symptom.

Kitchen sink clogs are their own category.

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom ones. Cooking fat cools inside the trap and horizontal run, then collects food scraps until the pipe narrows to a straw. A garbage disposal can mask the problem for months, because it pushes ground waste forward where it lodges in the next elbow. If the sink drains slowly and the disposal hums normally, the blockage is beyond the disposal, and our garbage disposal repair page explains when the unit itself is the culprit.

We clear kitchen lines with a small cable machine sized for 1.5 and 2 inch pipe, then run water for several minutes to confirm the line carries a full flow. If grease has coated the walls, a higher-pressure approach such as hydro jetting scours the film away instead of boring a hole through it.

How a drain call works.

Describe it.

Tell us which fixtures drain slowly and what happened first. That tells the plumber what to bring.

Locate it.

We test fixtures in order from the highest to the lowest to find where the blockage starts.

Clear it.

Cable or jetter, matched to pipe size, material and blockage type.

Confirm it.

Run water through every affected fixture and show you the flow before we leave.

Why Burnsville lines clog.

Much of Burnsville was built quickly in the 1970s after Interstate 35 arrived, according to the Star Tribune's history of the city. Fifty-year-old lateral lines in older neighborhoods are more likely to have rough joints, offset couplings and root intrusion than newer pipe. A repeat clog in a 1970s home is usually a pipe condition, not bad luck, and a camera pass tells you which.

Blockages in the city main look similar from inside the house. The City of Burnsville asks residents with a sewer backup to call Public Works at 952-895-4550 on weekdays and to call 9-1-1 evenings and weekends so crews can be dispatched. If every fixture in the house is stopped, check with a neighbor, and call us if the problem turns out to be only yours. Our sewer camera inspection shows which side of the property line the blockage sits on.

The standard for drain lines.

Drain, trap and vent requirements come from the Minnesota Plumbing Code, Minnesota Rules chapter 4714. A drain that was installed without proper venting or slope will clog again no matter how often it is cleaned, which is why we check the cause as well as the blockage.
