Every page below covers one kind of plumbing problem, with the signs to look for, what the plumber checks and what a repair involves. Pages for urgent problems say so at the top and tell you what to do while you wait.

If you are not sure which page fits, call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. The dispatcher can point you to the right service.

Plumbing work in Minnesota is held to the state plumbing code, Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, and each service page names the standard that applies to that kind of work.

Most emergencies fall into a handful of pages. A burst pipe or a flooded basement belongs with the leak and pipe services.

A backed-up toilet or a bubbling floor drain belongs with the drain and sewer services. No hot water belongs with the water heater pages. The groups below follow the same logic.
